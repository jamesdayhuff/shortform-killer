Shortform Killer

Oh, were you about to
watch just one?

Adorable. Forty minutes later you'd surface blinking, unable to remember a single thing you saw, wondering where the evening went.

The vertical feed isn't content, it's a slot machine. Every swipe is a pull, and the payout is a little squirt of dopamine for doing absolutely nothing. Do it long enough and ordinary things — a book, a conversation, a video with a second act — start to feel unbearably slow, because you've spent months training your attention to expect a reward every eight seconds.

That's not a personality trait you were born with. It's a habit, and habits are reversible.

Prescribed instead

5 pushups. Right now, on the floor, before you touch this keyboard again.

Same dopamine. Earned this time. Takes eleven seconds.

No skip button. That was rather the point.
